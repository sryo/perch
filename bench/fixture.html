Notes

The river rose two meters overnight and the lower path is closed until the water drops. Crews expect to reopen it by the end of the week, weather permitting.

Volunteers meet at the north gate at eight. Bring gloves, water and a hat; tools are provided on site. The morning group clears the trail and the afternoon group plants along the bank.

Tasks

  • Clear fallen branches from the upper trail
  • Replace the sign at the fork
  • Count the birds at the pond
  • Refill the first-aid kit
  • Photograph the washed-out culvert
